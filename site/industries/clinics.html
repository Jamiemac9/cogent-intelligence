01Where the week goes

We work with dental practices, physiotherapy clinics, aesthetics clinics, osteopaths, chiropractors and private healthcare. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Phones and enquiries. Reception juggles calls, walk-ins and treatment questions at the same time.
  • Cancellations and no-shows. Late cancellations leave gaps that nobody has time to fill.
  • Recalls. Check-ups, treatment courses and top-ups rely on someone remembering to send reminders.
  • Pre-appointment admin. Forms, consent and medical history collected at the last minute.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

An empty slot at 9am is lost forever. A message to the waiting list at 8:02 isn't.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Sending standard reminders
  • Filling cancellations from the waiting list
  • Updating booking notes

Asks first

  • Replies about treatment or prices
  • Anything involving money or refunds
  • Messages to unhappy patients

Never

  • Clinical advice or diagnosis
  • Discussing results

Patient data is special-category data. It's only handled by AI models approved for it, and clinical advice is never automated. Your CQC and GDC obligations stay with your clinicians.

08Questions

Can AI answer phone and web enquiries for a dental practice?

It can answer web, email and social enquiries with approved information and offer appointments. Anything clinical goes to your team.

How do I reduce no-shows and fill cancellations?

Reminders cut no-shows, and a Cancellation Filler messages your waiting list as soon as a slot opens.

Does it work with Dentally, Cliniko or Pabau?

Most practice systems can be connected. Where they can't, an agent can work them as a receptionist would.

Is patient data safe?

It's only processed by AI models approved for personal data, access is logged, and nothing clinical is automated.
