01Where the week goes

We work with architects, architectural technologists, interior designers and planning consultants. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Unqualified enquiries. Many enquiries have no budget, no site or no planning chance. Finding out takes a site visit.
  • Fee proposals. Each one is written from scratch against RIBA stages, scope and exclusions.
  • Planning tracking. Checking council portals for validation, consultee comments and decision dates.
  • Coordination. Chasing structural engineers, surveyors and clients for information.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

A practice's most valuable hours are design hours. Everything else should come to you ready to approve.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Checking planning portals
  • Logging enquiries and documents
  • Drafting proposals, updates and chasers

Asks first

  • Anything sent to a client, consultant or council
  • Fees and variations
  • Design decisions

Never

  • Making design or regulatory judgements
  • Signing off drawings

Design, building regulations and professional judgements stay with your architects. Agents handle the admin around them.

07Works with the tools you already use

We connect to what you have rather than asking you to move. Common tools in this sector include Monograph, Archisoft, Asana, Trello, Outlook or Gmail, Xero, Dropbox, SharePoint and council planning portals. If a system has no integration, a Portal Operator agent can work it the way a person would.

08Questions

Can AI write fee proposals for architects?

It drafts them from your rates, RIBA stages, scope and exclusions. You review and approve before anything goes to the client.

Can AI track planning applications?

Yes. A Planning Watcher checks council portals daily and reports validation, comments and decision dates.

Will it help us filter out time-wasting enquiries?

It asks the right questions about site, budget and planning before anyone books a visit.

Is it useful for a small practice?

Yes. One- to five-person practices often lose the largest share of their week to admin.
