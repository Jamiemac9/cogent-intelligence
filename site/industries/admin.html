01Where the week goes

We work with office managers, operations teams, PAs, virtual assistants and any business with a busy back office. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Double data entry. Typing the same details into the CRM, accounts and a spreadsheet.
  • Inbox overload. Shared inboxes where nobody is sure who's replying.
  • Document chasing. Signatures, forms and approvals that sit in someone's inbox.
  • Reporting. Pulling numbers from four systems every Monday morning.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

If a job is done the same way every time, a person shouldn't be doing it every time.

08Questions

Can AI do data entry between two systems?

Yes. Where systems connect, agents move data directly. Where they don't, a Portal Operator keys it in as a person would and checks it.

Is an AI admin assistant better than a virtual assistant?

They work well together. Agents take the repetitive work around the clock, and people handle judgement and relationships.

Can AI manage a shared inbox?

It labels, routes and drafts replies, and nothing is sent without approval until you've set it to.

Will it replace my admin team?

It removes the repetitive part of their week. Most teams use the time for work that was always being put off.
