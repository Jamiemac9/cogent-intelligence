01Where the week goes

We work with mortgage advisers, protection advisers and general insurance brokers. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Document chasing. Payslips, statements and ID arrive in pieces.
  • Product expiries. Retention depends on contacting clients early.
  • Case updates. Clients and agents want progress updates.
  • Lead follow-up. Protection and remortgage leads.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

Retention is won 90 days before a product expires.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Logging documents
  • Drafting reminders

Asks first

  • All client messages
  • Anything about products

Never

  • Recommending products

Regulated advice is never automated. Every client-facing message is approved by an adviser, and actions are logged for your compliance file.
