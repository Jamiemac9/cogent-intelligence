01Where the week goes

We work with plumbers, electricians, heating engineers, builders, roofers and kitchen and bathroom fitters. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Missed enquiries. Calls and web forms arrive mid-job. By the time you ring back, they've booked someone who answered.
  • Quotes written at night. Every quote means digging out measurements, photos and last year's prices, usually after a full day on site.
  • Chasing money. Invoices go overdue and chasing them is awkward, so it slips until cash gets tight.
  • Supplier and diary juggling. Orders, deliveries and site visits live in texts, emails and your head.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

The job that wins work is the first reply. Most trades lose it because they're busy doing the work.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Reading and labelling enquiries
  • Drafting replies and quotes
  • Updating job notes and the diary

Asks first

  • Anything sent to a customer
  • Any price, quote or invoice
  • Changes to a booked job

Never

  • Promising a start date you haven't confirmed
  • Agreeing a discount

Gas Safe, NICEIC and building-control paperwork stays with you. Agents can prepare the documents, but certificates and compliance sign-offs always wait for a qualified person.

08Questions

Can AI answer enquiries for my plumbing or building business?

Yes. An Enquiry Responder replies to web forms, emails and messages in minutes, asks your qualifying questions and offers survey times. While approvals are on, every reply waits for your tap before it goes.

Can AI write quotes for tradesmen?

It can draft them. The Quote Drafter builds a quote from your price list and survey notes, but nothing goes to a customer until you've checked and approved it.

Will it work with Tradify, ServiceM8 or Jobber?

Usually, yes. Most job management apps connect directly. If one doesn't, a Portal Operator agent can work it the way a person would.

Is it worth it for a one-person trade business?

Often it's sole traders who gain the most, because every hour of admin is an hour off the tools. The free call tells you whether your numbers stack up.
