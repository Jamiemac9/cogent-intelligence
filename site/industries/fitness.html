01Where the week goes

We work with independent gyms, boutique studios, personal trainers and online coaches. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Lead follow-up. DMs and ad leads arrive while you're coaching and need a reply quickly.
  • Trial bookings. Booking, reminding and following up after the trial.
  • Retention. Members who stop showing up rarely say anything before they cancel.
  • Client check-ins. Weekly check-ins, programme updates and payment reminders for PT clients.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

Most cancellations start weeks earlier, with a member who quietly stopped booking.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Sorting and labelling leads
  • Sending booking reminders
  • Collecting check-ins

Asks first

  • Messages to new leads
  • Prices, offers and refunds
  • Programme or nutrition changes

Never

  • Medical or injury advice
  • Agreeing a cancellation outside your terms

Health questions and injury advice always go to a qualified coach, never to an agent.
