01Where the week goes

We work with supported and exempt accommodation providers, supported living services and domiciliary care providers. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Evidence is scattered. Support plans, key-work sessions and risk assessments live in different systems and paper files.
  • Housing benefit claims. Claims, reviews and change-of-circumstance forms need chasing with councils and residents.
  • Rotas and cover. Shifts, sickness and cover requests change daily.
  • Audit preparation. Pulling evidence together before an inspection takes days of senior staff time.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

Being audit-ready isn't a project before the inspection. It's a habit your records keep every day.

06Where approvals sit

Every agent starts on a short lead. Here's a typical starting point for this sector. Yours is written into your Business Map.

Does alone

  • Filing and labelling records
  • Flagging gaps against support plans
  • Drafting reminders for staff

Asks first

  • Anything sent to a council, commissioner or resident
  • Changes to a support plan
  • Rota changes that cost money

Never

  • Safeguarding decisions
  • Clinical or care judgements

Resident data is special-category personal data. It's only processed by AI models approved for it, access is logged, and safeguarding, care and clinical decisions are never automated.

08Questions

How can supported housing providers prepare for an audit?

Keep evidence filed against each support plan as it happens. An Evidence Keeper does that daily and flags gaps, so preparing for an audit means opening a folder rather than building one.

Can AI help with housing benefit claims for exempt accommodation?

It can prepare forms, track every claim and chase councils and staff for missing evidence. Submissions always wait for your approval.

Is AI safe for resident data?

Resident data is only handled by AI models approved for personal data, every action is logged, and nothing involving safeguarding or care decisions is automated.

Does it work for domiciliary care providers?

Yes, especially for rotas, cover requests, visit notes and CQC evidence.
