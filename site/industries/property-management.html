01Where the week goes

We work with landlords, HMO operators, block managers and property management companies. The details differ from firm to firm, but the same few jobs come up on almost every discovery call:

  • Maintenance triage. Tenants report repairs by text, email and phone. Working out what's urgent and who to send takes hours.
  • Certificate deadlines. Gas safety, EICR, fire risk assessments and licences each have dates. Miss one and you're exposed.
  • Rent and arrears. Checking payments and sending reminders is slow and uncomfortable.
  • Contractor follow-up. Chasing quotes, access and completion photos from contractors.

None of these need your expertise. They need doing quickly, the same way every time, and that's exactly the kind of work AI agents do well, with your Business Map telling them how your business runs.

Key idea

Compliance failures rarely come from not caring. They come from a date living in someone's head.

06Where approvals sit

  • Logging and sorting repair reports
  • Tracking certificate dates
  • Drafting tenant and contractor messages

Asks first

  • Instructing contractors or approving spend
  • Anything sent to a tenant or leaseholder
  • Arrears and notice letters

Never

  • Serving legal notices
  • Deciding on a deposit dispute

Section 21/8 notices, deposit decisions and licensing applications stay with you or your solicitor. Agents keep the dates and the evidence ready.

08Questions

Can AI handle maintenance requests from tenants?

Yes. It collects the details and photos, judges urgency against your rules and drafts the contractor instruction for your approval.

How do I keep track of gas safety and EICR certificates?

A Certificate Tracker watches every expiry date, books inspections ahead of time and files the new certificate, so nothing depends on memory.

Does it work for HMO landlords?

Yes. HMOs have more certificates, more residents and more repairs, so the time saved is usually higher.

Can it chase rent arrears?

It sends polite reminders and logs replies. Formal arrears letters and notices always wait for you.
