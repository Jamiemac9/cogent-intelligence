Privacy notice

01Who we are

This website is run by Cogent Intelligence ("we", "us"), based in Solihull, West Midlands. We decide how the personal data described here is used, which makes us the data controller. Contact us at hello@cogentintelligence.co.uk or on WhatsApp at +44 7753 226214.

02What we collect

  • Booking form: your business name, industry, team size, the jobs taking your time, roughly how many hours they take, your name and, if you choose, your email address.
  • Calendar bookings: when you book through Cal.com, your name, email, chosen time and any notes you add.
  • Email course: your first name, email address and industry.
  • Messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp or email, including your phone number.
  • Analytics (only if you accept): pages visited, rough location (city level), device and browser type, and which buttons were used, collected by Google Analytics.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

The booking form doesn't store your answers on our servers. They travel with your calendar booking or your WhatsApp message, and only when you choose to send them.

03Why we use it

  • To arrange and prepare for your discovery call and send your one-page summary. Lawful basis: steps you've asked us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries.
  • To send the email course and occasional emails. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it. Lawful basis: your consent through the cookie banner.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

04Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and routes our email.
  • Cal.com runs our booking calendar.
  • Google provides Google Analytics (only with your consent) and serves the site's fonts.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries any messages you send us there.
  • Our email provider sends the email course.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the UK-US data bridge or adequacy regulations.

05How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your enquiry details within 12 months of our last contact. Course subscribers stay on our list until they unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as the contract runs and for six years afterwards for legal and accounting reasons.

06Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the foot of every page. The cookie policy lists every cookie we use.

07Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, ask us to restrict it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@cogentintelligence.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

08Client data

When we build agents for a client, we process their customers' data on the client's instructions as a data processor. That's covered by a separate data processing agreement, signed before any agent goes live.
