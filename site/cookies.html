Cookie policy

Which cookies we use, why, and how to change your mind. Last updated 12 October 2026.

On this page

  1. In short
  2. Cookies and storage we use
  3. Changing your mind
  4. Third-party sites

01In short

We use one optional set of cookies, from Google Analytics, and only if you accept them. Everything else the site stores is there to make it work, and it never leaves your device.

02Cookies and storage we use

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
_gaAnalytics (optional)Google Analytics: tells visits apart2 years
_ga_4MVYR1TFDBAnalytics (optional)Google Analytics: keeps track of your current visit2 years
cogent-consentNecessary (local storage)Remembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear it
cogent-seenNecessary (session storage)Skips the intro animation after your first pageUntil you close the tab
cogent-focusPreference (local storage)Remembers your reading-mode setting on articlesUntil you clear it

03Changing your mind

Use the "Cookie settings" link at the foot of any page to accept or reject analytics. If you reject them after accepting, we delete the Google Analytics cookies straight away. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.

04Third-party sites

Our booking calendar (Cal.com) and WhatsApp open on their own sites when you click through, and they set their own cookies under their own policies. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which doesn't set cookies.
